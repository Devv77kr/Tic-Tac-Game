** Tic‑Tac‑Toe is a two‑player, turn‑based paper-and-pencil game played on a 3×3 grid. Players alternate placing their mark (X or O) in empty cells. The first player to place three of their marks in a straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) wins. If all nine cells are filled without either player forming such a line, the game is a draw.
Rules & setup

Board: 3 rows × 3 columns.
Players: 2 (one is X, the other O). X usually goes first.
Move: On your turn, place your mark in any empty cell.
Win condition: Three of the same mark in a row, column, or diagonal.
Draw condition: All cells filled and no winner.
Illegal moves: Placing a mark in an occupied cell — not allowed.
Typical notation

Cells often numbered 1–9 or addressed as (row, col). Example layout: 1 | 2 | 3 —+—+— 4 | 5 | 6 —+—+— 7 | 8 | 9
Basic strategy highlights

Best opening: take the center (cell 5) if available.
Corners are stronger than edges; prefer corners when center is taken.
Always block opponent’s immediate winning move.
Force forks (two simultaneous threats) or prevent opponent’s forks.
With perfect play by both sides, Tic‑Tac‑Toe ends in a draw.
